## Refunds: read the small print

This is where the providers differ most.

- **IPTV Kingdom:** 3 days, first-time subscriptions only. Its refund policy also says you lose the refund if you used the free trial before buying.
- **BritCast:** 7 days if a fault on its side cannot be fixed within 48 hours (another section of the same page says 24 hours).
- **GB IPTV:** the homepage promises a 24-hour money-back guarantee, but the refund page only covers accounts that cannot be activated.
- **Televo IPTV:** no number of days. Refunds are "reviewed individually".
- **IPTV United Kingdom:** 7 days if the service does not work on your device and support cannot fix it, paid back within 5 working days.

If a provider's homepage and refund page disagree, ask them which one applies **before** you pay, and keep the answer.
